Yes — Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST) is a strong Buffett-style business. It has a durable competitive advantage, predictable customer demand, recurring membership revenue, and consistently high returns on shareholders' equity.

## Costco's ROE — Last 5 Fiscal Years

Using Costco's SEC Form 10-K filings for fiscal years 2022–2026:

| Fiscal year | Net income | Average shareholders' equity | ROE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2022 | $5.844B | $19.103B | 30.59% |
| 2023 | $6.292B | $22.850B | 27.54% |
| 2024 | $7.367B | $24.340B | 30.27% |
| 2025 | $8.099B | $26.393B | 30.69% |
| 2026 | $9.226B | $32.484B | 28.40% |

Five-year average ROE: **29.50%**.

The response also displayed a chart labelled “Costco ROE by fiscal year — Return on average shareholders' equity, using SEC annual reports.”

Calculation: ROE = Net Income / Average Shareholders' Equity × 100.

Average equity is the average of beginning-of-year and end-of-year equity, rather than just year-end equity.

Sources: Costco's [2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm) and [2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983224000049/cost-20240901.htm).

## Why Costco qualifies as a Buffett business

- Economic moat: Costco's enormous purchasing power allows it to offer competitive prices that smaller retailers struggle to match.
- Recurring revenue: Membership fees reached $5.91 billion in 2026, with a 92.3% renewal rate in the U.S. and Canada.
- Consistent profitability: ROE stayed above 27% throughout the five-year period.
- Strong brand loyalty: Its membership model and Kirkland Signature products encourage repeat purchasing.
- Understandable business: Costco makes money through high-volume retail sales and membership fees rather than complicated financial products.

One qualification: Costco paid a roughly $6.66 billion special dividend in 2024, reducing shareholders' equity and affecting subsequent ROE calculations.

Verdict: Costco passes the Buffett-style business-quality test. Its competitive advantages and consistently high ROE make it an attractive long-term business. However, Buffett also emphasizes purchasing at a reasonable valuation. A great business is not automatically a great investment at any stock price.
